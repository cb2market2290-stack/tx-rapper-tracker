# Research Notes: Bitrue Micro-Cap Coins

## What to look for
- Coins with sudden volume spikes (3-5x normal)
- Coins with positive 24h change AND high volume = real buying pressure
- Coins near recent all-time highs (momentum continuation)
- New listings on Bitrue (fresh coins, price discovery phase)
- Utility tokens with real use cases not just speculation

## Red flags
- High volume but flat price = sell pressure absorbing buys
- Price up but volume low = weak move, likely to reverse  
- Too far from target (>100,000x needed) = very high risk
- No trading pairs elsewhere = harder to exit

## Wealth strategy
- Small position sizes across multiple Fire/Strong coins
- Take profits at 5x, 10x, 50x — dont wait for the full 1000x
- Set stop losses — micro-caps can go to zero fast
- Track in projects/bitrue-analyzer/watchlist.md
